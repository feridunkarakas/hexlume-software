Hewrune Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. This policy describes how HexLume Software ("we") handles information in the game Hewrune on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

You do not need to create an account. To provide the online leaderboard and nicknames, the game creates an anonymous guest profile on our server (hosted on Supabase). It stores: a randomly generated anonymous user ID and player ID (RH-XXXXXX), the nickname you choose, your leaderboard scores (reached chapter, highest Tower floor, Battle Power, strongest boss defeated), your equipped look and items shown on the leaderboard, your device platform ("iOS" or "Android") and the app version. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location or contacts.

Advertising

Hewrune shows optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for extra rewards. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay with ad networks such as ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. These partners may collect device information, such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions and basic diagnostics, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

iOS: the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android: the Android Advertising ID may be used. You can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run the leaderboard, keep nicknames unique, show and measure ads, and improve the game. We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and deletion

Game progress is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Leaderboard data (anonymous user ID, player ID, nickname, scores and leaderboard look) is stored on our server until you ask us to delete it.

How to request deletion: email feridunkarakas@gmail.com with the subject "Hewrune data deletion" and include your player ID (RH-XXXXXX, shown in the game). We will delete your leaderboard profile and all scores linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. We do not keep any of this data after deletion. Data collected by our advertising partners is handled under their own policies.

Children

Hewrune is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
