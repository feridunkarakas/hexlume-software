Hewrune Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026. This policy describes how HexLume Software ("we") handles information in the game Hewrune.

Information we collect

You do not need to create an account. To provide the online leaderboard and nicknames, the game creates an anonymous guest profile on our server (hosted on Supabase). It stores: a randomly generated anonymous user ID and player ID (RH-XXXXXX), the nickname you choose, your leaderboard scores (reached chapter, highest Tower floor, Battle Power, strongest boss defeated), your equipped look and items shown on the leaderboard, your device platform (for example "iOS") and the app version. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location or contacts.

Advertising

Hewrune shows optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for extra rewards. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay with ad networks such as ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. These partners may collect device information, such as your IP address and, if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt, the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), to show and measure ads. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use information

We use the information above only to run the leaderboard, keep nicknames unique, show and measure ads, and improve the game. We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and deletion

Game progress is stored on your device. Leaderboard data is stored on our server. To delete your leaderboard profile, email us your player ID and we will delete it.

Children

Hewrune is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
