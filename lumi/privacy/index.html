Lumi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026. This policy describes how HexLume Software ("we") handles information in the game Lumi: Lantern Logic Puzzle on iOS and Android.

Information we collect

You do not need to create an account. Lumi does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location or contacts, and does not send your game progress to our servers. Progress, settings and purchases are stored on your device only.

Advertising

Lumi shows optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for extra rewards. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay with ad networks such as ironSource Ads. These partners may collect device information, such as your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device identifiers, ad interactions and basic diagnostics, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

iOS: the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android: the Android Advertising ID may be used. You can reset or delete it at any time in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple on iOS and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use information

We use advertising-related information only through our ad partners, to show and measure ads. We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and deletion

Game data is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. We keep no user data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data collected by our advertising partners is handled under their own policies. Questions or requests: email feridunkarakas@gmail.com.

Children

Lumi is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
